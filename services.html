Research design & experiments

Designing and running studies on music perception, memory, and learning, in the lab or online at scale.

study and experimental design · online testing

Statistical modelling & data analysis

Turning behavioural and audience data into clear answers, with models suited to the question.

statistical modelling · machine learning · visualisation

adaptive training · web apps · data products

psychology of music · playing by ear · methods
