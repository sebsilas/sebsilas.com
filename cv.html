Dr.Seb Silas

Postdoctoral Researcher · Data Scientist · Programmer · Lead Lecturer
sebsilas@gmail.comgithub.com/sebsilaslinkedin.com/in/seb-silassebsilas.com

Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Systematic Musicology, University of Hamburg. My research investigates how musical information is perceived, remembered, learned, and transmitted, combining music psychology, cognitive science, systematic musicology, and computational modelling. Alongside academic research, I work as a data scientist and musicologist for SoundOut and teach Psychology of Music at BIMM University London.
